PRIMARY MATHEMATICS CURRICULUM GUIDANCE

Class 4 Mathematics

Unit: Everyday Arithmetic Money (C4-U18)

Money in Class 4 should help pupils understand value, exchange, and simple transactions using realistic contexts.

Pupils should connect money problems to addition, subtraction, and practical reasoning.

Fractions in Class 4

Fractions should be introduced through visual models and everyday sharing contexts before symbolic manipulation.

Teachers should avoid introducing unrelated secondary topics when teaching fractions.
