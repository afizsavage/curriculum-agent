MBSSE BASIC EDUCATION CURRICULUM FRAMEWORK

Section Mathematics Education

The purpose of mathematics education in Sierra Leone is to develop numeracy, reasoning, and problem-solving skills that pupils can apply in daily life.

Mathematics teaching should emphasize conceptual understanding, fluency, and the ability to communicate mathematical ideas clearly.

--- PAGE 2 ---
Primary Level Mathematics

At the primary level, mathematics should connect classroom learning to everyday contexts such as money, measurement, and data.

Teachers should use structured progression from concrete experiences to abstract reasoning.

--- PAGE 3 ---
Science Education

Science education at primary level should develop curiosity, observation, and evidence-based explanation.

This section provides general guidance and does not replace syllabus learning outcomes.
